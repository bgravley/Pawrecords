YourPetPass Health Records Team

The YourPetPass Health Records Team creates practical guides that help pet parents organize veterinary information, prepare portable medical records and make important pet information easier to access when routines are disrupted.

Transparency: YourPetPass Health Records Team is an organizational editorial identity, not an individual person or licensed veterinary professional. Health and preparedness guides are educational and are researched against primary public-health, government and veterinary sources where appropriate.

What we cover

Pet health-record organization, vaccination and medication documentation, emergency preparedness, portable medical summaries, veterinary-record workflows and owner education.

What we don't claim

Our guides do not diagnose, prescribe, replace veterinary care or create emergency-management requirements. For medical questions, treatment decisions and prescription planning, consult your veterinarian.
