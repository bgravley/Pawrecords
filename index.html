YourPetPass — Your Pet's Health Records, Organized and Travel-Ready

YourPetPass is a pet health records and travel planning app built for dog and cat parents. It keeps vaccination history, vet visit records, allergies, and medications in one place — accessible no matter which veterinarian you see, in any city.

Built for pet parents who move

Whether you've switched vets, relocated, or travel frequently with your dog or cat, YourPetPass keeps every health record attached to your pet, not to a single clinic.

AI-powered travel checklists

Create a route-specific AI-assisted planning checklist with official-source links. Country entry, export, transit, quarantine, health, vaccination, treatment, permit, and customs rules should point to the responsible government authority. Carrier sources are used for carrier-specific policy. Confirm current requirements before travel.

AI document scanning

Take a photo of a vet record, vaccine certificate, or health document and YourPetPass can use AI to extract useful details. The extracted information is presented for you to review before you rely on it or save it to your pet's profile.

Emergency QR health card

If you enable Emergency QR for a pet, it creates a tokenized, no-login emergency page. You choose which supported emergency fields are shared. It is not a public pet directory and does not expose the pet's full medical record or private uploaded documents.

Pricing

YourPetPass offers a free plan for core health record storage. Premium plans start at $4.99/month and include AI document scanning, AI travel checklists, weight tracking, document storage, and the QR emergency health card. Annual plans are $39.99/year and a Lifetime plan is available for $89.99.
